1. Who we are

Haven is made by Company legal name, registered address (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

2. What the app does with your files

Haven reads the folders you choose so it can search, sort and clean them up. To do that it keeps an index on your Mac with file names, sizes, dates, where downloads came from, and text it reads from your files (for example, words in screenshots, PDFs and transcribed recordings).

  • The index is stored in ~/Library/Application Support/Haven on your Mac. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Text recognition, background removal, transcription, translation and summaries run on your Mac using Apple's frameworks and the Whisper model. Your files are not uploaded to do any of this.
  • File tools write new files next to your originals. Files Haven moves go to the Trash, where you can restore them.
  • The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting service, advertising or tracking code.

You can delete the index at any time by deleting the Haven folder in ~/Library/Application Support.

3. When the app goes online

Haven connects to the internet only for these things:

  • Speech models. If you use Transcribe, Haven downloads the Whisper model you choose from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which hosts the open-source whisper.cpp models. Hugging Face receives the request, including your IP address, as any website would. Nothing about your files is sent.
  • Updates. Haven checks our update server for new versions. The request includes the app version and your macOS version, and our server sees your IP address.
  • License activation. When you enter a license key, Haven sends the key and a random identifier for this Mac to our licensing service so we can count activations. It does not send your name, files or anything about them.

4. When you buy a license

Payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. Stripe collects your payment details directly and handles them under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email address, billing country and postal code, the plan you bought, the price and tax, and a record of the payment. We never see or store your full card number.

We use this to deliver and manage your license, send your receipt and important notices about your purchase, handle refunds and meet our tax and accounting obligations. We do not send marketing email unless you ask for it.

5. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it loads no fonts or scripts from other companies. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (IP address, pages requested, browser type) for security and reliability, for no longer than 30 days.

6. When you write to us

If you email us, we keep your message and our reply so we can help you and follow up. Please don't send files you'd rather keep private; we'll never need them to help with your license.

7. Who we share information with

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run Haven, under contracts that limit what they can do with it:

  • Stripe, for payments, receipts and tax calculation.
  • License service provider, for license keys and activations.
  • Email provider, for receipts and support email.
  • Hosting provider, for this website, downloads and update checks.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it. We never sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

8. How long we keep it

We keep purchase records for as long as tax and accounting law requires (typically up to 7 years), license records for as long as your license is active, and support email for up to 3 years after your last message. Then we delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the EU, UK and California, you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, receive a copy, or object to how we use it. Write to us at the address below and we'll answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on performing our contract with you (to sell and support your license), our legal obligations (tax records) and our legitimate interest in keeping our services secure.

Some of our providers are in the United States. Where information leaves the EU or UK, it is protected by safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Children

Haven is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in the EU), and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change matters, for example a new kind of information we collect, we'll say so on this page and, if you've bought Haven, by email, before it takes effect.
