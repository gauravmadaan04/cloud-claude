      Questions, answered.

      Is Haven a subscription?

      No. You pay once and Haven is yours to keep. Every update to version 1 is free. If there's ever a Haven 2, the version you have keeps working.

      Does Haven replace Finder?

      No, it works alongside Finder. Your files stay in the folders they're already in. Haven indexes them so you can find them, clean up and work on them faster, and any file is one shortcut away from Finder.

      Are my files uploaded anywhere?

      No. Search, text recognition, transcription, background removal and every file tool run on your Mac. There's no account and no analytics. Haven goes online only to download a speech model if you ask for transcription, check for updates and activate your license.

      Could Haven delete something I need?

      Haven never deletes anything outright. Clean Up and Duplicates always show you the files first. When you confirm, they go to the Trash, and Undo puts them back. File tools never change your originals; they make new files next to them.

      Which Macs does it run on?

      Any Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon or Intel. Translate needs macOS 15 or later, and Summarize needs a Mac with Apple Intelligence on macOS 26.

      What happens when the trial ends?

      Your files stay exactly where they are and nothing is lost. Haven asks for a license to keep going. Buy one and paste the key, and everything carries on as before.
